Surabhi is a Data Engineer at American Express, working in the Global Risk and Compliance Technology space, where she builds large-scale data platforms for customer risk scoring and rating systems. She specializes in designing and optimizing distributed data pipelines using Apache Spark, Kafka, and cloud-native GCP technologies such as BigQuery, Airflow, Dataflow, and Pub/Sub to process and analyze massive volumes of data with high reliability and performance. Her work focuses on converting complex risk and compliance data into actionable insights that support regulatory adherence, enterprise-wide risk management, and data-driven decision-making at scale.

Beyond her core role, Surabhi is deeply engaged in the tech ecosystem. She is a GCP Certified Data Engineer and Cloud Architect, a winner of prestigious hackathons including the Smart India Hackathon, HackViolet International hack and Amex Makeathon and was also a speaker at KCD Delhi 2026, reflecting her passion for innovation and problem-solving.
