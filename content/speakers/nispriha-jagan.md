I am an Associate Software Engineer at Red Hat, working with the OpenShift Node team on kubelet and CRI components. I contribute to Kubernetes upstream across sig-node and sig-storage. I have worked on KEP-5855 (bind mount options on volumeMounts) and KEP-5502 (emptyDir volume permission mode), both targeting Kubernetes v1.37. I have also worked on KEP-6035 (exec session identity propagation) and actively contribute to CRI-O. I am enthusiastic about cloud technologies and scalable backend systems.
