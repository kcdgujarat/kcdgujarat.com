Hrittik is a Platform Advocate at vCluster Labs and a CNCF Ambassador with expertise in cloud native technologies and open source communities. He has contributed extensively to developer advocacy, technical writing, and community engagement. Hrittik has been a keynote speaker at events such as the MCP Dev Summits and has delivered talks at Kubernetes Community Days, Open Source Summits, and more. He has also served as a Program Committee member for several KubeCons and CloudNativeCons.
