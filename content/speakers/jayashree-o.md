Jayashree is a Software Engineer II at Guidewire with over 3 years of experience building secure, cloud-native platforms and developer infrastructure. Her work focuses on cloud platforms, software supply chain security, and improving the security and visibility of modern software delivery pipelines.

She has contributed to internal platforms for SBOM analysis, dependency intelligence, and secure software delivery. She has hands-on experience across the container build ecosystem, including Cloud Native Buildpacks and kpack, and has actively contributed to GUAC (Graph for Understanding Artifact Composition), where she has worked on deploying, querying, and leveraging the graph to enhance software supply chain insights.

Jayashree is passionate about open source, cloud-native technologies, and building secure-by-design developer platforms.
