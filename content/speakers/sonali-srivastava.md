Sonali Srivastava is a Senior Developer Advocate at Improving, co-chair of KubeCon India 2026, and an organizer at the CNCF Women in Cloud Native Community Group. With over seven years of experience spanning system administration, software development, and developer advocacy, she has established herself as a prominent voice in the open source and cloud-native communities.

Sonali focuses on bridging the gap between developers and CNCF projects by creating POCs for cutting-edge technologies and developing engaging blogs, demos, tutorials, and videos. She is the creator of LFS255: Mastering Kubernetes Security with Kyverno, a comprehensive course hosted by The Linux Foundation.

She has spoken at major conferences, including KubeCon India, SOSS Community Day, KCD Bengaluru, FOSS United Chennai, and Mumbai chapters. She has made significant contributions to the open source project systemd during her Outreachy internship.

Based in Mumbai, India, Sonali welcomes connections from students and caregivers looking to start or restart their careers in technology, embodying her commitment to giving back to the community that has supported her journey. She is dedicated to creating supportive environments for underrepresented groups in technology.
