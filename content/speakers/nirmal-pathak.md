Nirmal Pathak is a Cloud Infrastructure Architect, AWS User Group Vadodara Leader & AWS Community Builder. He has more than 20 years of professional experience in Linux Administration, Cloud Infrastructure, DevOps and Platform Engineering. As the leader of the AWS User Group Vadodara, Nirmal is deeply committed to mentoring engineers and driving the adoption of modern, cloud-native engineering patterns like workload-first infrastructure scaling and advanced observability.
