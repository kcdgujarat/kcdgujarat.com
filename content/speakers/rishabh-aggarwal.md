Rishabh Aggarwal is a Solutions Architect at Coredge.io, working on sovereign cloud and enterprise platform delivery. He has extensive experience across cloud infrastructure, Kubernetes platform engineering, and GPU-based AI infrastructure, with a focus on identity, security, and disaster recovery for government and enterprise clients. His work spans multi-site cloud architecture, GPU-based AI Cloud & Inference platform design, and privileged access systems. He's passionate about the cross-cutting problems in infrastructure that don't fit neatly into any single layer of the stack.
