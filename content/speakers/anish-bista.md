He is a Kubernetes Engineer specializing in DevOps, cloud technologies, Kubernetes, Golang, and the broader cloud-native ecosystem. The youngest Kubestronaut from Nepal and an active contributor to the CNCF and open-source ecosystem, he has played a key role in growing the CNCF Kathmandu community and has contributed to projects such as KubeVirt and Kanister. He currently maintains the KubeVirtBMC project and is an LFX mentee for the KubeVela project under CNCF. He is an accepted speaker at COSCUP 2026, representing KubeVirtBMC in Taiwan.
