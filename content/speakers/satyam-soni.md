Satyam Soni is a Dev Advocate at NitroStack, where he works with enterprises and developers building MCP servers, agentic applications, and developer platforms. His work spans AI and cloud-native technologies, helping teams adopt MCP and agent-native architectures in production.

Satyam is a CNCF Ambassador, former K8s Release engineering, and have served as Release Notes Lead for Kubernetes v1.32. He has spoken at KubeCon, PyCon, OSS Summit, opensearchcon and developer conferences worldwide.
