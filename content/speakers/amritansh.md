I am an associate software engineer at Red Hat, working with the OpenShift Node team on kubelet and CRI components. I contribute to Kubernetes upstream across sig-node , I am the author of KEP-5999 (HTTP/2 cleartext container probes) and KEP-4939 (TLS support for gRPC probes), all targeting Kubernetes v1.37. I also contribute to CRI-O and have worked on kubelet bug fixes, probe handling, and memory QoS improvements in upstream Kubernetes. I am enthusiastic about cloud technologies and scalable backend systems.
