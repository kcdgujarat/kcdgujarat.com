Ishan Mahadevia is a DevOps Tech Lead and Platform Engineer with extensive experience designing and operating large-scale cloud-native platforms. His work focuses on Kubernetes, observability, and platform reliability, with a particular interest in running Prometheus and Grafana Mimir at enterprise scale.

He has built and operated centralized observability platforms supporting hundreds of Prometheus servers across Kubernetes and bare-metal environments, helping engineering teams improve reliability, scale metric collection, and simplify operations. His experience includes Prometheus fleet management, long-term metrics storage, OpenTelemetry, and building practical monitoring and alerting strategies for production systems.

Ishan enjoys sharing real-world engineering lessons, mentoring fellow engineers, and contributing to the cloud-native community through technical content and conference talks.
