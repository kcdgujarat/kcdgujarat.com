Raghu is a Staff Platform Engineer (Security) at Calix Inc. , where he secures Kubernetes infrastructure powering AI and MLOps workloads across multiple clusters.

With over 10 years in platform engineering, security and Kubernetes, his work focuses on the gap most organizations ignore. Applying supply chain security, runtime hardening, and policy enforcement to ML pipelines that are often the most privileged and least protected workloads in a cluster.

He is organizer for Cloud Native Hyderabad Chapter and has spoken at KCD Bengaluru 2025, KCD Kochi 2026 and CNCF community meetups
