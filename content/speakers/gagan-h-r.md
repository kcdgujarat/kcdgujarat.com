Gagan is a Software Engineer at Guidewire, building scalable, cloud-native applications across the stack. He is an active open source contributor within the Cloud Native Computing Foundation (CNCF) and the Open Source Security Foundation (OpenSSF), with over 200 contributions to the open source community. His work includes contributions to GUAC to help enhance software supply chain security, alongside hands-on experience using and exploring tools like Kyverno, as organisations navigate growing risks around dependency management, provenance, and trust in open source software. He is also a active contributor to Confidential Containers CNCF project. Gagan brings deep technical expertise in Kubernetes and Go-based backend engineering to his work across the cloud-native ecosystem.
