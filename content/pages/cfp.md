# Session Topics

For KCD Gujarat, we broadly align with the KubeCon + CloudNativeCon ecosystem tracks while also encouraging submissions that reflect the realities of engineering and open source adoption across India and the wider community.

Suggested topic areas include:

- AI Inference + Agentic
- Application Development + Delivery
- Cloud Native Experience
- Cloud Native Novice
- Connectivity
- Data Processing + Storage
- Emerging + Advanced
- Observability
- Operations + Performance
- Platform Engineering
- Security

## We also welcome

- Real-world production stories
- Failure case studies & lessons learned
- Open source community experiences
- Accessibility & inclusive technology
- AI/ML workloads on Kubernetes
- Platform engineering journeys
- Beginner-friendly educational content

Avoid sales or marketing pitches, product demonstrations disguised as talks, or sessions centered around proprietary product promotion. Vendor-neutral, educational, and community-focused talks are strongly preferred.

# Session Formats

We welcome a variety of session formats designed to support both deep technical learning and community interaction.

## Lightning Talk — 10 minutes

Short, focused presentations or quick technical demos showing a concept, tool, experiment, or community project in action.

Great for:

- Quick lessons learned
- Emerging ideas or experiments
- Open source tools and demos
- Community stories

## Session Presentation — 25 minutes

Standard conference session format focussed on technical deep dives, architecture case studies. production lessons learned, best practices, ecosystem knowledge sharing, or operational insights.

Recommended structure: ~20 minutes presentation + ~5 minutes Q&A.

Limited to 1–2 speakers.

## Panel Discussion — 25 minutes

A moderated discussion among multiple speakers exploring a focused topic from different perspectives.

Panels work best when the topic is sharply focused, speakers bring varied experiences, and the moderator keeps discussion structured and engaging. 

Recommended structure: ~20 minutes discussion + ~5 minutes audience Q&A.

Limited to 2–4 panelists plus 1 moderator.

To encourage diverse representation:

- Panels with 3+ participants should include at least one gender-diverse speaker
- Participants should not all be from the same company

## Workshop — 90 minutes

Hands-on, interactive sessions where attendees actively build, configure, deploy, troubleshoot, or practice something tangible.

Workshops should:

- Clearly explain prerequisites
- Include interactive participation rather than lecture-style presentations
- Provide attendees with actionable takeaways
