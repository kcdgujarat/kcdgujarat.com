Kubernetes Community Days (KCD) Gujarat is Gujarat's first CNCF-backed, community-organized conference dedicated to Kubernetes, Cloud Native technologies, DevOps, Platform Engineering, Observability, Security, AI/ML Infrastructure, and Open Source. It brings together developers, operators, platform engineers, students, maintainers, and technology leaders to learn, network, and share real-world experiences.
