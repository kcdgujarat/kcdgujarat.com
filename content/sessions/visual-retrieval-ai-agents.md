Text embeddings flatten your PDFs into tokens and lose what matters: tables, charts, layout. VultronRetriever solves this by reading rendered pages as visual data, creating dense embeddings that capture the complete context. In this talk, we'll explore how visual retrieval powers modern AI agents. We'll see how Hermes agents orchestrate the retrieval flow, wire it through MCP, and deliver grounded, cited answers from your actual documents. We'll show you how conversational agents understand your knowledge base, and break down the complete flow from document ingestion to answered questions.
