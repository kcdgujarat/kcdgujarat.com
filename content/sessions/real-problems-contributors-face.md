Contributing to open source isn't just about getting started. It's about staying in the game.

You finally understand the codebase. You're writing PRs. And then reality hits: your PR gets rejected. The project moves at light speed, and you can't keep up. You're joining 3am calls from your timezone just to sync with the team. And now everyone's using AI to code so you used it too, but you feel like you're cheating. These aren't beginner problems. They're the real problems contributors face when they're actually trying to stick around.

In this talk, I'll explore questions we all ask, and we'll discover we have the same answers. I'll share experiences from top contributors and my own journey, then ask the room, 'Did this happen to you too?' We'll see how many of us hit the same walls in the same places.

It's just honest. And we all feel the same.

Takeaways:

- The real problems intermediate contributors face (rejection, burnout, imposter syndrome)

- That you're not alone

- How to survive when the project moves faster than you
