The cloud-native movement in India has taken off like a rocket! We’re filling auditoriums at KubeCons and KCDs, dominating global GSoC and LFX cohorts, building indigenous startups, and scaling mission-critical systems worldwide. But let’s be honest, it’s not always a walk in the park, and our rapid growth story comes with its fair share of growing pains!

This talk is all about taking a real, unfiltered look into the trenches of India’s cloud-native ecosystem. We’ll dive into what’s working: the silent heavy lifters, cross-community alliances, mentors sponsoring travel out of pocket, and incredible engineers representing India on global stages. But guess what? We’re also here to tackle the ongoing struggles head-on: the heavy toll of volunteer burnout, corporate teams consuming open source without giving back, and those transactional, low-effort PRs that risk hurting our reputation upstream.

These challenges are a natural part of maturing, and we’re here to uncover the common ground we all share. With practical insights from community leaders, maintainers, and builders, we’ll explore what the future of our ecosystem can look like - a world where we move beyond being the world’s largest consumer base to becoming a sustainable, respected community of maintainers and leaders. Join us to see how we can make India's cloud-native movement not just huge, but truly world-class for the next generation!
