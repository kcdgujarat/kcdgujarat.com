Every packet of Amul butter carries milk pooled from thousands of different farmers, tested at thousands of different points, and somehow becomes one of the most trusted brands in the country without a second thought.

That trust was not built recently, and it was not built by accident. It was designed almost 80 years ago, decades before anyone was talking about AI agents, protocols, or Kubernetes, and it still holds today.

This session asks a simple question: if a cooperative built to move milk across thousands of untrained, independent people already solved the trust problem, why does it still feel unsolved in cloud native AI?

We will trace how Amul actually earns that trust, one tier at a time, from the village level testing that decides what enters the system, to the storage that keeps it usable, to the layer that decides what gets certified and shipped.

Each tier maps to something running in production today, from a protocol like MCP, to a vector store like Milvus, to an agent framework like kagent, solving the same problem cloud native AI is still working out.

Come find out how an eighty-year-old dairy cooperative already solved a problem most AI stacks haven't.
